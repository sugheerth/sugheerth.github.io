  \item
    \projectheading{A Constraint Aware Framework For Safe Sampling}
    \resumeItemListStart
      \resumeItem{Developing a deterministic safe sampling framework that integrates a closed-form trajectory-level CBF correction directly into diffusion-model sampling updates for fast, safety-aware trajectory generation.}
      \resumeItem{Formulated a single smooth, trajectory-level CBF by aggregating obstacle- and waypoint-level constraints through nested softmin approximations, enabling a closed-form safety correction that applies weighted updates across waypoints according to their relative safety. }
      \resumeItem{Derived a first-order DPM-Solver discretization of the safety-augmented probability-flow ODE and delayed CBF corrections until the final denoising steps to reduce local-trap formation.}
      \resumeItem{Demonstrated 100\% trajectory safety across the evaluated Maze2D settings and a 91\% safe-success rate in the dense PointMass2D environment, while generating trajectories in 0.05–0.07 seconds.}
